# ThaaLam Support

ThaaLam is an offline metronome and tala practice companion for iPhone, iPad and
Android.

## Quick help

### I cannot hear the click

- Check the media volume, not just the ring volume.
- Make sure the play button is showing the stop icon.
- If Bluetooth headphones are connected, confirm they are the active output.
- If you use a silent switch, ThaaLam still plays through the media channel; an
  active Focus mode or another audio app can still take focus.

### The count-in is silent or longer than expected

Open **Practice** on the metronome screen and check the count-in bar count. For a
tala with an eduppu, the count-in is phase-aligned to the requested start point,
so the first click may not be on beat 1.

### How do I export my practice history?

Open the **Practice** tab, scroll to **Export**, and choose CSV or JSON. The file
is shared through the system share sheet. Nothing is uploaded.

### How do I clear practice history?

Open **Practice**, choose **Clear** in Recent sessions, and confirm. Goals and
presets are kept.

### Tap accuracy seems consistently early or late

Open **Settings → Tap calibration** and adjust the value. The engine estimates
output latency, but Bluetooth routing and device speakers can still differ.

### The click drifts or stutters

ThaaLam schedules clicks from rendered audio frames, not a UI timer. If you hear
a problem, please send the device model, iOS/Android version, audio route
(speaker, wired, Bluetooth) and what changed.
